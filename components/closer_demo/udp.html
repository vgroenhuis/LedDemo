About

UDP sends single packets: nothing is retried or kept in order, unlike the WebSocket (TCP) the demo page uses. A lost packet is simply gone, and a late one doesn't hold up the next. Round trip = there and back again. The echo counts what it receives, so a lost packet can be told apart: lost on the way out or on the way back.
