Steady: connection quality, green good, yellow fair, red poor. The worst of signal (−60 to −82 dBm), lost pings (0 to 20 %) and ping time (20 to 200 ms) counts. A short dark blip is a lost ping.
Blue, breathing: looking for the network
Cyan, fast blink: joined, getting an address
White flicker on the colour: looking for a stronger access point; white fast blink: moving to it
Orange blink: connection lost, reconnecting
Red flash every 1.5 s: no connection for over 30 s
Purple double flash: the network refused the password
